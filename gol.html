Controls

Basic Controls:

◉ Click on a cell to flip its value.

◉ You can hold click and draw across the screen.

◉ Move the camera with ASWD or arrow keys.

◉ Zoom the screen with scroll wheel or the "+" and "-" keys.

◉ Press space to start/stop the simulation.

◉ Hold Shift to highlight cells and use various hotkeys on them.

Basic Hotkeys

◉ When paused, use the "t" key to advance 1 tick; Use Y to advance TPT ticks.

◉ Command+S to save the current state.

◉ R key to load saved state.

◉ I key to place a pixel at a distance from mouse.

◉ Command R to fully clear board.

Highlight Hotkeys (all require Command key held)

◉ RightClick on any selected cell to drag the selected and reposition it.

◉ C: Copy

◉ V: Paste (hold down v to see where it will be placed)

◉ D: Rotate 90 Degrees (scuffed)

◉ "," Flip X

◉ "." Flip Y

◉ DeleteKey: Delete selected cells

Editing Hotkeys

◉ F key to place/remove a colored marker at location

◉ Command G key to delete all markers placed

◉ N key to switch between marker colors
